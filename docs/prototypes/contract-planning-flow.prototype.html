Claudia · Mun Lander II on the padCareer · Mission Control lvl 2

Walkthrough

  1. Clear window. Optionally type your own words (try “Also collect science from Kerbin orbit”).
  2. Start Mission: the mod reads your 7 accepted Contracts (no Claude).
  3. The Contract check card grows in at the bottom. Tick, untick or Swap in: it re-prices in place.
  4. Plan these: the card folds to a summary; Claudia's plan proposal is the first LLM request.
  5. Each leg carries the Requirements it serves (tags from the mod's plan check). Approve.
