# site_hop from the ground, and why land_suborbital went wrong (#1355, for #1316)

Human check #1316 asks whether `land`, given a craft on a suborbital arc that ends near its site, flies its powered descent onto the site with no orbit first. Neither `land_suborbital` nor `site_hop_mun` starts where a Site Hop really starts, because the block-test runner could spawn the craft under test only in orbit. It can now also spawn it landed: `start = landed` with `landed_body`, `landed_lat` and `landed_lng` in a `test.cfg`. `land` also reports `started_suborbital` (true when `LANDSTART` answered "arc"), so a test can check it directly. The agent run that wrote this did not launch KSP.

**Why `land_suborbital` failed (2026-11-24):** its `test.ks` called `exec_node(lexicon("stage_limit", 2))`. The lander's only engine is in stage 0, and a craft spawned with nothing fired is at stage 1, so it was already at or below the limit of 2. `exec_node` could not stage into the engine and returned at once without burning. The log shows this: `exec_node` made no warp request, and `LANDSTART` answered `orbit` 3 s after the start. `land` then started from orbit with the test's unflown node still on the flight plan. Both `land_suborbital` and `site_hop_mun` now call `exec_node()` with the default stage limit.

**Block test to fly:** `tests/blocks/site_hop_landed_mun`. `tests/blocks/land_suborbital` is worth one more run now that its burn can light.

1. **It starts on the ground, at rest.** (#1358, #1363) The start the test asks for, 2.46, 77.2, slopes 24.5 degrees. Before spawning, the runner searches out from it, to 10 km, for the nearest ground under 5 degrees, read as `land` reads slope, and then does the same for the hop's target, 2.46, 81.5, keeping the hop within 2 km of the 15 km asked for. `KSP.log` shows one line for each: `block test: moved the landed start from 2.46, 77.2 to <lat>, <lng>, <n> km away, where the ground slopes <n> degrees` and `block test: moved the hop target from 2.46, 81.5 to <lat>, <lng>, <n> km away, where the ground slopes <n> degrees` (or `the landed start at 2.46, 77.2 slopes <n> degrees: it stays there`, and the same for `the hop target at 2.46, 81.5`, when no move was needed), then `block test: the hop is <n> km, 15.0 km asked for`. If there is no such ground within 10 km, the test fails before spawning: `block test: FAIL site_hop_landed_mun: no ground under 5 degrees within 10.0 km of the landed start at 2.46, 77.2: the flattest found slopes <n> degrees`, or for the target `... of the hop target at 2.46, 81.5 that keeps the hop within 2.0 km of the 15.0 km asked for: the flattest found slopes <n> degrees`. Report that line: the site needs moving by hand. Otherwise `landed 'Claudia lander test' on Mun at <lat>, <lng>` shows, at the moved start, the lander is set down 0.2 m above the ground, and once it is unpacked `block test: waiting for 'Claudia lander test' to settle: LANDED under 0.1 m/s for 3 s, at most 30 s` follows. When it has stood still for 3 s, `block test: settled: LANDED and still, leaning <n> degrees on ground sloping <n> degrees` shows, then `running site_hop`. If it never settles, the test fails before the script starts: `block test: FAIL site_hop_landed_mun: never settled: 30 s without 3 s LANDED under 0.1 m/s; the craft leans <n> degrees on ground sloping <n> degrees`. Then `site_hop_landed test: start LANDED at <lat>, <lng>` and `site_hop_landed test: slope <n> at the start, <n> at the site` print. If either slope is 5 or more, or it topples, report both slopes and a screenshot.
2. **The hop is planned from there.** `block call SITEHOP` lines read 13 to 17 km, near the hop the runner logged. If the site is dark at touchdown, the craft first warps on the ground. It rises a few metres, pitches east, burns, cuts and coasts on a short arc.
3. **land takes over on the arc.** `land`'s `block call LANDSTART` line reads `arc ending <n> m from the aim`, not `orbit`. No `phase pass` or deorbit node follows, and the powered descent brings it down near the moved hop target.
4. **It passes.** `PASS site_hop_landed_mun`, every `ok` line: `test_start` LANDED, `success` true, LANDED, `started_suborbital` true, `hop_km` 13 to 17, `test_start_slope` and `test_site_slope` under 5, `test_hop_m` under 5000, touchdown under 3 m/s, lean under 15 degrees.
