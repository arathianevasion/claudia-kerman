# site_hop from the ground, and why land_suborbital went wrong (#1355, for #1316)

Human check #1316 asks whether `land`, given a craft on a suborbital arc that ends near its site, flies its powered descent onto the site with no orbit first. Neither `land_suborbital` nor `site_hop_mun` starts where a Site Hop really starts, because the block-test runner could spawn the craft under test only in orbit. It can now also spawn it landed: `start = landed` with `landed_body`, `landed_lat` and `landed_lng` in a `test.cfg`. `land` also reports `started_suborbital` (true when `LANDSTART` answered "arc"), so a test can check it directly. The agent run that wrote this did not launch KSP.

**Why `land_suborbital` failed (2026-11-24):** its `test.ks` called `exec_node(lexicon("stage_limit", 2))`. The lander's only engine is in stage 0, and a craft spawned with nothing fired is at stage 1, so it was already at or below the limit of 2. `exec_node` could not stage into the engine and returned at once without burning. The log shows this: `exec_node` made no warp request, and `LANDSTART` answered `orbit` 3 s after the start. `land` then started from orbit with the test's unflown node still on the flight plan. Both `land_suborbital` and `site_hop_mun` now call `exec_node()` with the default stage limit.

**Block test to fly:** `tests/blocks/site_hop_landed_mun`. `tests/blocks/land_suborbital` is worth one more run now that its burn can light.

1. **It starts on the ground, at rest.** (#1358) The lander toppled at the first site, 2.46, 77.2, so the test now starts at 1.153, 79.031 in East Crater, where mission f623's `land` touched down upright, and hops to 1.153, 83.33. Its slope there has not been read in KSP yet. Before spawning, the runner reads the start's slope as `land` does. Over 5 degrees it refuses, and `KSP.log` shows `block test: FAIL site_hop_landed_mun: the landed start at 1.153, 79.031 slopes <n> degrees, more than 5: move it to flatter ground`, with no spawn after it. Otherwise `landed 'Claudia lander test' on Mun at 1.153, 79.031` shows, the lander is set down 0.2 m above the ground, and once it is unpacked `block test: waiting for 'Claudia lander test' to settle: LANDED under 0.1 m/s for 3 s, at most 30 s` follows. When it has stood still for 3 s, `block test: settled: LANDED and still, leaning <n> degrees on ground sloping <n> degrees` shows, then `running site_hop`. If it never settles, the test fails before the script starts: `block test: FAIL site_hop_landed_mun: never settled: 30 s without 3 s LANDED under 0.1 m/s; the craft leans <n> degrees on ground sloping <n> degrees`. Then `site_hop_landed test: start LANDED at 1.153, 79.031` and `site_hop_landed test: slope <n> at the start, <n> at the site` print. If it topples, report that with a screenshot.
2. **The hop is planned from there.** `block call SITEHOP` lines read about `15 km`. If the site is dark at touchdown, the craft first warps on the ground. It rises a few metres, pitches east, burns, cuts and coasts on a short arc.
3. **land takes over on the arc.** `land`'s `block call LANDSTART` line reads `arc ending <n> m from the aim`, not `orbit`. No `phase pass` or deorbit node follows, and the powered descent brings it down near 1.153, 83.33.
4. **It passes.** `PASS site_hop_landed_mun`, every `ok` line: `test_start` LANDED, `success` true, LANDED, `started_suborbital` true, `hop_km` 14 to 16, `test_start_slope` and `test_site_slope` under 5, `test_hop_m` under 5000, touchdown under 3 m/s, lean under 15 degrees.
5. **Report on #1316:** pass or fail, and the `LANDSTART` line, the first and last `SITEHOP` lines, `hop_burn_dv`, `test_hop_m`, `touchdown_speed_actual`, `dv_used`, the `settled:` line and both slopes. If it fails, also send the `land trace` lines and the `block test: FAIL` lines.
