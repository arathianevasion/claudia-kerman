# A craft that can enter comes home by direct entry (#981)

In mission m20261110-203402-45d1 (a Duna round trip, the craft carrying an Entry Shield and an Mk16 on the pod), the per-turn `plan_transfer` row for Kerbin said "arrival: 1125 of 1711 m/s: target_pe 80000, capture(), deorbit(), reentry()", because the delta-v covered a capture. Claudia planned the capture (1,718 m/s against 671 for a direct entry), circularized at 262 km and ran short. Now `HomeLanding.Arrival` takes a capture first only when the craft can't enter as it is (`EntryCraft.CanEnter`: an Entry Shield, and chutes or an engine). A capture asked for (`capture` true) is still priced.

1. **Fly a Duna round trip** with a craft that has an Entry Shield under the pod and a parachute, and a Mission Goal like "land on Duna and return to Kerbin" that names no place on Kerbin. Let Claudia plan as usual.
2. **The Kerbin row.** Once in Duna orbit (or at the leg's Mission Plan request), the stage table's `plan_transfer {"target":"Kerbin","target_pe":80000}` row in the request (the transcript's user message) has `"capture":false`, no `capture_dv` in its windows, and `"arrival_note":"arrival: entry at 21 km, no capture burn; shielded: transfer_to, reentry()"`. Its `total_dv` is the ejection alone (about 670 m/s from low Duna orbit).
3. **The plan.** The Mission Plan's Kerbin leg is budgeted at about that figure, with no "capture into orbit" or "deorbit" leg, and its Sequence Plan flies `transfer_to` to Kerbin then `reentry`. A capture leg in Claudia's plan fails this check, unless the Mission Goal or the player asked for an orbit first.
4. **It comes home.** The craft enters Kerbin's air from the transfer, the shield takes the heat, and the chutes land it. Say on #981 whether it did, with the Kerbin row's note.
5. **No shield keeps the capture.** Optional: the same trip with a craft that has no Entry Shield and more than enough delta-v still gets "capture(), deorbit(), reentry()" in the Kerbin row.
