# A burn starts without empty radials (#985)

On the Duna round trip (m20261110-203402-45d1) the Kerbin capture flew a 950.8 m/s node with the empty radial stages still on: 2.8 t of dry FL-T400s, heat shields, nose cones and legs. #979 lets the "auto" drop take a radial set with a shield (and legs once no landing is left). Now exec_node also runs that drop **before** its burn, after the turn check and before it stages into its engines, so every block that burns through it (`capture`, `transfer_to`'s ejection and corrections, `circularize`, `change_orbit`, `tune_pe`, `deorbit`, `return`) starts its burn without tanks that are already empty. During the burn the drop runs as before. Tanks with propellant left, and a stack decoupler over the pod's Entry Shield, stay.

1. **Reload the craft on its way home.** In `~/KSP_spike/saves/Claudia Test 2/Backup/`, `persistent (2026_11_10_16_47_17).sfs` has *Duna Ship 1* on its Sun-centred path to Kerbin, 56 parts, with the four radial pods (radial decoupler, FL-T400, heat shield, nose cone, landing legs) still on. Copy it over a scratch save, never the original. In the tweakables, check that the four radial FL-T400s hold no fuel (the crossfeed drained them on the Duna ascent). If one still holds fuel, note it: that one must stay.
