# Empty radials with heat shields or legs drop (#979)

On the Duna round trip (m20261110-203402-45d1) the craft lifted off Duna with four empty radial FL-T400s and carried them home: each radial pod had a heat shield, and after the player's redesign landing legs too, and the "auto" drop only let empty tanks and trim go. Now a heat shield no longer blocks an auto drop **when a radial decoupler takes it**, and landing legs don't once Core (`AutoDrop.LegsFree`, asked through `ADDONS:CLAUDIA:AUTODROPFREE`) finds no landing left in the Mission Plan from the sequence in hand on. Engines, parachutes and crew still block, and with no Mission Plan (a block test) legs still block.

- **The crew part's Entry Shield never goes.** A shield a stack decoupler would take (pod, decoupler, shield, tank; or pod, decoupler, tank, shield) holds that decoupler as a kerbal would: only a radial decoupler (`ModuleAnchoredDecoupler`) lets a shield go, as Core never counts a radial shield as an Entry Shield (#843).
- **Named drops.** `drop_empty` with a list skips the kinds check, as before. A part under a shield leaves with the radial decoupler above the shield; under a stack decoupler it has no decoupler of its own, so naming the tank in pod, decoupler, shield, tank fires nothing (as before #979). In pod, decoupler, tank, shield, naming the tank still fires the decoupler and the shield goes with it, as it did before #979: a named drop is the player's word, and it doesn't check for crew either.
- **A landing** is a sequence whose words say land or touch down, deorbit, reenter or splash down, or descend / descent. A landing on Kerbin is never one, whatever its words, by the player's ruling: a sequence that comes down at Kerbin or a place on it (KSC, the Kerbal Space Center), or returns home, is no landing on its own. "Return to KSC", "Reentry at Kerbin", "Land at KSC", "Reenter over KSC", "Descend into Kerbin's atmosphere", "Deorbit and splash down on Kerbin", "Return to Kerbin and reentry ... then reenter and land" and "Return to Kerbin and touch down at KSC" all leave the legs free. It is one only when it comes down on another stock body it names: land, touch down, splash down, deorbit or descend in the same clause before the body, or after the body before the next reentry word or Kerbin place, and not in that Kerbin place's own clause past the body's ("Leave Mun orbit, then land at KSC" lands on Kerbin; "Mun landing and Kerbin return" lands on the Mun) (a reentry word doesn't stop it when a return follows the landing: "Duna: reenter and land on the plains, then return to Kerbin") ("Deorbit to the Mun crater, then return to Kerbin and land" and "Leave Mun orbit and land at the crater, then return to Kerbin" keep the legs). Otherwise the body is where the return comes from ("Return from the Mun to Kerbin and land", "Leave Mun orbit, then reenter and land" and "Eject from Duna orbit at the soonest Duna-to-Kerbin window ... then reenter and land" leave the legs free). A sequence that doesn't come down at a Kerbin place and doesn't return home ("Touch down", "Duna entry", "Descent to Duna equator", or one that only mentions Kerbin: "Land in the crater with Kerbin overhead", "Land; transmit to KSC", "Touch down during Kerbin-rise") is a landing, as before. The name and intent read as separate sentences.
