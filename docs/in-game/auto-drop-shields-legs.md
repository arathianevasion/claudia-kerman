# Empty radials with heat shields or legs drop (#979)

On the Duna round trip (m20261110-203402-45d1) the craft lifted off Duna with four empty radial FL-T400s and carried them home: each radial pod had a heat shield, and after the player's redesign landing legs too, and the "auto" drop only let empty tanks and trim go. Now a heat shield no longer blocks an auto drop **when a radial decoupler takes it**, and landing legs don't once Core (`AutoDrop.LegsFree`, asked through `ADDONS:CLAUDIA:AUTODROPFREE`) finds no landing left in the Mission Plan from the sequence in hand on. Engines, parachutes and crew still block, and with no Mission Plan (a block test) legs still block.

- **The crew part's Entry Shield never goes.** A shield a stack decoupler would take (pod, decoupler, shield, tank; or pod, decoupler, tank, shield) holds that decoupler as a kerbal would: only a radial decoupler (`ModuleAnchoredDecoupler`) lets a shield go, as Core never counts a radial shield as an Entry Shield (#843).
- **Named drops.** `drop_empty` with a list skips the kinds check, as before. A part under a shield leaves with the radial decoupler above the shield; under a stack decoupler it has no decoupler of its own, so naming the tank in pod, decoupler, shield, tank fires nothing (as before #979). In pod, decoupler, tank, shield, naming the tank still fires the decoupler and the shield goes with it, as it did before #979: a named drop is the player's word, and it doesn't check for crew either.
- **A landing** is a sequence whose words say land or touch down, deorbit, reenter, or descend / descent. A return to Kerbin under chutes ("Return to KSC": a return home that doesn't say it lands) is not one, unless it also names another stock body as somewhere it goes ("Deorbit to the Mun crater, then return to Kerbin" keeps the legs; "Return from the Mun to Kerbin" doesn't).

1. **The Duna ascent drops the radials.** Fly the player's redesigned craft (radial pods: radial decoupler, FL-T400, heat shield, nose cone, landing legs) on a Mission with the same plan words as this flight's (land at Duna, crew report, ascend from Duna, transfer to Kerbin, return to KSC). A save on the Duna surface before "Ascend from Duna" is enough: copy it over a scratch save, never the original. When the radials run dry during the ascent, all four leave. `KSP.log` has `block call AUTODROPFREE ... free shield, leg` (at most once a second), and the launch result's `dropped` names the radials' parts. Fail: the ascent ends with the radials still on, or `free shield` alone.
2. **Not before the landing.** On the same craft, earlier in the Mission (the Kerbin launch, or the Duna transfer while "Land at Duna equator" is still to fly), the log's `AUTODROPFREE` line says `free shield` only. Empty radials with legs stay on; radials without legs (shield and nose cone only) go once empty.
3. **The pod keeps its Entry Shield.** In the VAB, build pod, stack decoupler, heat shield, small fuel tank (drained to 0 in the tweakables), engine-less, with chutes on the pod; launch it on a Mission whose plan has no landing left, with `drop_empty` "auto" running (any Burning block, e.g. a short `launch`). The decoupler under the pod never fires and the shield stays on; no `AUTODROPFREE` answer changes that. Fail: the shield leaves.
4. **The block tests still pass.** Run `tests/blocks/drop_empty` (a named drop: no `AUTODROPFREE` line, since only an auto drop asks) and `tests/blocks/launch_orbit` (the auto drop) as in their `test.cfg`. Both pass their checks. `launch_orbit`'s log has `AUTODROPFREE ... free shield` (no Mission Plan, so no legs).
