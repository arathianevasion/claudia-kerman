# tune_pe flies a tenth-of-a-m/s burn far out (#982)

On the way home from Duna, 6.7 million s before Kerbin, `tune_pe(lexicon("value", 80000, "patch", "next", "max_dv", 150))` solved the right burn, 0.0995 m/s, but exec_node's own stop (0.1 m/s left) was above the whole node: it ended before the engine lit, the trim did the same, and the block failed with the periapsis still 262,601 m. Now `BURNWORTH`'s `tune_pe_burn` answer caps a small burn's throttle and stops it once what is left would move the periapsis less than half the tolerance, down to one tick at the least throttle.

1. **Reload the flight's save.** In `~/KSP_spike/saves/Claudia Test 2/Backup/`, `persistent (2026_11_10_16_47_17).sfs` is the craft on its Sun-centred path to Kerbin before the corrective sequence (copy it over a scratch save, never the original). Run, as a Custom Script or a Mission Script, `local res is tune_pe(lexicon("value", 80000, "patch", "next", "max_dv", 150)). addons:claudia:report(res).`
2. **The log.** `KSP.log` has a `CORRECTIONBURN` line with a node of about 0.10 m/s, then `BURNWORTH ... fly it: throttle at most 0.013, stopping within 0.002 m/s`. The exec_node trace shows the engine lit at a low throttle for about a second.
3. **The trim lights too.** If the first burn leaves the periapsis outside 1 km, tune_pe trims with a second node of a few thousandths of a m/s. Its `BURNWORTH` line says `throttle at most 0.005` (the least throttle, never lower) and stops within about 0.002 m/s. Its exec_node trace must show `burn_time` above 0, a `node_dv` of a few thousandths, and a `residual` of about 0.002 m/s or less: it ended on the stop or on the overshoot check. A trim that fails "max_duration", or shows `burn_time` 0, fails this check.
4. **It burns.** The report's `pe_actual` is no longer 262,601: it is near 80,000. The pass line is **within about 11 km of 80,000**, not the 1 km tolerance: far out, 0.001 m/s moves this periapsis about 1.8 km, one physics tick at the least throttle is about 0.002 m/s (about 3.5 km), and one pass of exec_node's loop may span 2 or 3 ticks (about 7 to 11 km). So a success, or a failure "over tolerance 1000 m from 80000 m; encounter safe" with `pe_actual` within about 11 km of 80,000, both pass; 262,601 again, or `dv_used` 0, fails it. Say which on #982, with the trim's trace.
5. **The block tests still pass.** Run `tests/blocks/tune_pe_kerbin` (patch current) and `tests/blocks/tune_pe_mun` (patch next to the Mun) as in their `test.cfg`; both pass their checks. Their burns are several m/s, so the throttle stays full; the stop may be nearer than 0.1 m/s when the tolerance asks for it.
