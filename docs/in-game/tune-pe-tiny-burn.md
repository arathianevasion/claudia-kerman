# tune_pe flies a tenth-of-a-m/s burn far out (#982)

Now `BURNWORTH`'s `tune_pe_burn` answer caps a small burn's throttle and stops it once what is left would move the periapsis less than half the tolerance, down to one tick at the least throttle.

1. **Reload the flight's save.** In `~/KSP_spike/saves/Claudia Test 2/Backup/`, `persistent (2026_11_10_16_47_17).sfs` is the craft on its Sun-centred path to Kerbin before the corrective sequence (copy it over a scratch save, never the original). 2. **The log.** `KSP.log` has a `CORRECTIONBURN` line with a node of about 0.10 m/s, then `BURNWORTH ... fly it: throttle at most 0.013, stopping within 0.002 m/s`. The exec_node trace shows the engine lit at a low throttle for about a second.
3. **It burns.** The report's `pe_actual` is no longer 262,601: it is near 80,000 (the first burn leaves it within a few km; the trim within about 1 km).
