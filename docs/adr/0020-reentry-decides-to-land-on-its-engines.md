# reentry decides for itself to land on its engines

At block start, once #823 has said what the Entry would drop and #826 which chutes it would open, `reentry` predicts the touchdown with that drop and those chutes. At or under 10 m/s it flies on its chutes and drops as ADR 0018 says. Over 10 m/s with an engine that has fuel, it keeps everything, drops nothing, predicts again for the craft as kept, and lands on the engines under its Chute Group. Over 10 m/s with no such engine it takes the chute path, and #827 decides what else opens. Flight m20261106-203509-34a6 predicted "powered finish, 24.1 m/s" and its script stayed on chutes, since only the script's `touchdown` option could choose the engines. The player chose this rule on 2026-11-06.

- **`touchdown` is removed.** Giving it fails with a reason that says it is automatic, at the Script Check for an inline lexicon and in flight otherwise. `chutes: "none"` with an engine aboard lands on the engine.
- **Two speeds.** 10 m/s is this decision's threshold, allowing for prediction error (#682 is open). 15 m/s is the limit the Craft Check judges by and the block fails on, on engines too: the 6 m/s limit `touchdown: "powered"` had is gone, since a craft short of fuel would otherwise fail for that alone.
- **Lighting.** The block activates the engines part by part and fires no stage: `reentry` still never stages. It lights every engine with fuel in the highest stage aboard, whatever the current stage, as one of a cluster would turn the craft. If those flame out while braking it lights the other engines that had fuel. Thrust that doesn't appear after lighting is the chute path.
- **What counts as fuel.** Stock's flow (`Part.GetConnectedResourceTotals`, per propellant), so crossfeed and fuel lines count and no tank is summed. A throttle-locked engine doesn't count: a solid motor can't brake a landing, and lighting a Sepratron would fire it at once.
- **Shield.** A powered entry sets `separate` to `"none"`, so a shield with anything below it stays on, as its decoupler takes both sides. One with nothing below it goes at `reentry_shield_due`'s timing, or as braking starts if that comes first, before the engines fire.
- **Short of fuel.** It brakes with what it has, and only a touchdown over the limit fails the block.
- **In Core, firing in Kerboscript (ADR 0014).** `EntryFinish.Decide` is the decision, behind `ADDONS:CLAUDIA:ENTRYFINISH`, next to `CHUTEGROUP`. Braking, throttle and lighting stay in `reentry`. `keep_engines` and its walk (`EntryStack.Kept`) are deleted with their tests. The behaviour changes on purpose, so there is no parity test. The chute path in `Decide` is the seam #827 extends.

**The checks.** The Margin Check budgets `TouchdownSpeed` for a finish on any Entry but the return with a touchdown over 10 m/s and an engine aboard, as well as for any over 15 m/s, as before. Findings stay on 15 m/s: from 10 to 15 m/s with an engine the delta-v is carried in the margin and the Chutes line reads "powered finish", with no finding, as the chutes survive without the engines. No prompt text changes.

**Kept Blockers.** A Kerbin return its chutes can't cover stays a Blocker (#557: a stack on that entry runs close to the heat limit), though in flight the engines beat a crash. Chutes in one stage over two Entries stays a Blocker even when the first Entry lands on its engines, since that Entry opens the group all the same and the second has none. Only a script's `chutes: "none"` would save them, and the Craft Check can't see a script.

We rejected leaving the choice to the script, as the flight shows it gets missed, and keeping only the engines and the parts in line with them, as that dropped the radial tanks: about 3,264 of the flight's 3,338 m/s, and its stage-2 chutes.

Decided on #824 (2026-11-06).
