science(options): runs the craft's science experiments here (current body, situation and biome)
and keeps or transmits the data, one reading per experiment, on a free part first. Never
overwrites data, and never runs a part that is inoperable or has nothing to measure here.
Options:
experiments ["all_useful"]: "all_useful" runs every experiment that has science left here (the
addon knows how much); in Sandbox nothing is tracked, so it runs each experiment once. Or a
list of experiment ids ("temperatureScan") or part titles/names, run whether or not science
is left. A name that matches no experiment part fails the block.
transmit ["auto"]: "keep" leaves the data in the experiment; "transmit" sends it home, data a part
already holds too (it stays if there is no antenna or link; see kept); "auto" keeps it,
transmitting held data only to free a part when all hold some.
max_duration [1800]: seconds, transmission waits included.
plus min_ec_pct, abort_on_part_loss and stage_limit as for every block (it never stages).
Data is kept while electric charge is under min_ec_pct.
Extra results: results (a list with one lexicon per experiment part: experiment, part, action
"ran", "sent held data" or "skipped", detail, and transmit "transmitted", "kept" or ""), and
kept: hints for data kept for want of a link or charge, or "no free part" (none freed; fails the
block if named). Fails on an option it can't read, a name matching no experiment, or an abort.
Data left aboard comes home with the craft. Nothing to run is a success. EVA reports and samples
are the player's job.
Example: `local res is science(lexicon("transmit", "keep")). addons:claudia:report(res).`
